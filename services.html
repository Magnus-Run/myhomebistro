Our menu is curated around what is available seasonally. A typical sit-down lunch or dinner will be from 3 to 5 courses. We also pair the wine with the courses. A 3 course comes with one glass of wine and a 5 course comes with two glasses of wine.

We provide table linen, cutlery, glasses, plates and everything else needed on the table to make this experience a memorable one.
